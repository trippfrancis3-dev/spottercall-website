Live Atmospherics

Live Atmospherics from Your Kestrel

Pair once. Forget it's there.

SpotterCall reads live atmospherics from your Kestrel Link device over Bluetooth — temperature, pressure, humidity, density altitude — and feeds them straight to the firing solution. The wind shifts at 600. The temperature drops twenty degrees by dusk. The hold updates. You don't move off the rifle.

Compatible with any Kestrel Link-enabled meter — the Drop D3, the 5700 Sportsman, the 5700 Elite with WEZ. Pair your meter once in settings; from then on it's silent. The integration runs in the background; you stay in the workflow.

No retyping conditions. No copy-paste from a separate screen. No taking your eyes off the target to confirm a number.

Spotter AI is the conversational spotter — voice-to-voice, brain-on, reading the field with you.

Where SpotterCall calls back a hold, Spotter AI talks back. Ask it the wind. Ask it the drift. Ask it whether to wait out the gust. Ask it to remember your last shot and adjust for the next one. The conversation is the interface; the math runs underneath.

Reads the field through your sensors. Pair your Kestrel 5700 for live atmospherics — temp, pressure, humidity, density altitude — over Bluetooth. Pair a compatible wind meter and Spotter AI hears the downrange wind in real time. Conditions update; the hold updates; the call updates. You stay in the workflow.

Built for the way hunters actually shoot. Most hunters don't dial their scope; they hold over the target with a capped scope and a known reticle. Spotter AI's Hold Mode gives the call in inches-over-target — "hold seven inches high, three left" — not just mils. A pie-plate hold table tells you the truth about where the bullet lives at every distance for your load. "Your pie plate is 320 yards" is the confidence language a hunter actually needs.

Sits next to what you already trust. Spotter AI isn't asking you to replace your existing ballistic calculator or your range-day workflow. It rides alongside, voice-first, reading the same conditions you'd read manually — faster, hands-free. The shooter who trusts their existing ballistic calculator keeps trusting it. Spotter AI is the voice layer on top.
